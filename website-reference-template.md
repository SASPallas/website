# Website Reference Template

## Purpose

This document is the working content reference for the website. Each section should capture the raw strategic language, positioning, and message hierarchy that the site design and copy should express.

Answers can be rough. They do not need to sound final. The goal is to collect the underlying ideas first, then refine them into website-ready language.

## 1. Mission

**Prompt:** What is the mission of the company or website?

**Answer:** To provide university faculty and programs the space to understand how new technologies are used, challenge and engage with technology through dialogue and application, and bring that newfound knowledge back to the classroom.

## Values

- We believe students need critical thinking, systems design, and risk-taking to build fulfilling and productive careers.
- We believe education should prepare students to question, shape, and design the use of technology in everyday work.

**Earlier mission draft:** To help the next generation of college graduates capture the benefits of technological change through university-enterprise partnerships that expand agency, creativity, and shared prosperity.

**Raw source language:** We want to capture technological spillovers for the next generation of college graduates to enable shared prosperity and a humanist workforce. We actively engage with universities and enterprises to enable agency for the next generation of productive, creative, and technologically adept members of the workforce.

## 2. What We Do

**Prompt:** In plain language, what does the company do?

**Answer:** Pallas Analytics helps university faculty and programs understand how new technologies are being used in everyday work and bring that knowledge back into teaching, curriculum, and student preparation.

Core activities:

- Run an annual survey and white paper on the state of entry-level employment for white-collar jobs.
- Use on-the-ground knowledge about how the latest LLMs actually work in practice.
- Facilitate faculty deep-dives in universities, including for faculty who are not technical, so they can have a dialogue on how AI can be challenged and used.
- Run curriculum strategy sessions on how to integrate next-generation thinking into STEM education.

The work should be presented through distinct research and educational philosophy sections.

## 4. Core Problem And Point Of View

**Prompt:** What problem does this company solve, and why does it matter now?

**Answer:** There is a disconnect between the way companies want to use AI and how it is actually being used. Schools can get ahead of the game by understanding these new challenges.

Core problem themes:

- Entry-level work is changing quickly.
- Companies want to use AI, but there is often a gap between the stated ambition and how AI is actually used in practice.
- Schools need a clearer understanding of the new challenges so they can respond earlier.
- Faculty need practical knowledge of how the tools work so they can help students become more empowered.

Point of view:

- We believe fulfilling and productive careers will need to value critical thinking, systems design, and risk-taking.
- We do not want to simply create new tasks with the rise of automation, but broaden and challenge the ways we adopt and shape it.
- Universities play a critical role in driving the dialogue and challenging conventional or over-promised delivery of technology.

**Raw source language:** We believe fulfilling and productive careers will need to value critical thinking, systems design and risk-taking. We don't want to simply create new tasks with the rise of new automation but broaden and challenge the ways we will adopt and shape it. Therefore, we believe universities play a critical role in driving the dialogue, challenging conventional or over-promised delivery of technology.

## 5. Products Or Offerings

**Prompt:** What are the main products, services, or offerings that should appear on the website?

**Answer:** The website should present four main offerings:

### Annual Survey And White Paper

**Audience:** University career centers.

**What it does:** The annual survey and white paper examine the state of entry-level white-collar employment, including how work is changing, what skills are being valued, and where recent graduates are experiencing gaps.

Universities may be able to ask for specific targeting or a breakdown of their own alumni by college to see how their graduates compare against a benchmark and inform curriculum adjustments, if any.

**Outcome:** Career centers get tangible data on new graduate preparedness. College graduates who get jobs can support more admissions and more funding.

### Faculty AI And LLM Deep-Dives

**Audience:** Faculty groups across disciplines, including art faculty, data science faculty, and others who want to understand how AI is being used in the real world.

**What it does:** These sessions help faculty understand how AI is being used in real-world applications. Faculty need to be empowered about the technology in order to empower their students.

Faculty need to understand how to design new ways to use it and become critical users of it if we expect students to be empowered.

**Outcome:** Faculty should feel motivated and equipped to refresh their courses so they reflect a new and changing future of work.

### Curriculum Strategy Sessions

**Audience:** Department leadership.

**What it does:** Curriculum strategy is the most specific way for there to be a unified vision about how and what a department wants their students to come away with.

Pallas facilitates these strategy sessions, bringing real-world perspective and needs.

**Outcome:** Department leadership gets a unified vision for what they want students to come away with.

**Raw source language:** Survey: University career centers would need it. The deep-dives can be for art faculty, data science, etc. Really, it is to understand how AI is being used in application in the real world. Curriculum would be department leadership. Perhaps universities can ask for a specific targeting or breakdown of their own alumni by college to see how their graduates compare vs benchmark to inform curriculum adjustments if any. Faculty needs to be empowered about the technology in order to empower their students. They need to understand how to design new ways to use it and become critical users of it if we expect the kids to be empowered. Curriculum strategy is the most specific way for there to be a unified vision about how and what a department wants their kids to come away with. We facilitate these strategy sessions bringing the real-world perspective and needs.

### Foundation Partnerships

**Audience:** Foundations with educational resources, including arts and culture philanthropy.

**What it does:** Pallas creates interdisciplinary opportunities for artists and writers to be upstream of technology and not become disenfranchised by it.

This is based on the Director of ML role at Chanel Arts and Culture philanthropy and their needs.

**Outcome:** Foundations get a way to support artists and writers in shaping technology earlier, instead of reacting to it after the fact.

**Raw source language:** For foundations, it is based on the Director of ML role at Chanel Arts and Culture philanthropy and their needs. We uniquely create interdisciplinary opportunities for artists and writers to be upstream of technology and not become disenfranchised by it.
