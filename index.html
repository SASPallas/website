Research, prototypes, and applied systems

Building small systems to understand large changes.

Pallas Analytics is my working archive for prototypes at the edge of AI, judgment, data, and institutional change. The projects here are experiments in making complex decisions more visible: regulated model review, GxP execution, publishing intelligence, climate-to-sensory risk, and the future of work.

What this is

A landing page for research directions, working prototypes, and the product questions behind them.

The through-line is not a single product. It is a way of working: take an ambiguous domain, build a concrete interface, test what data and architecture can support, and use the prototype to learn what the real problem is.

Future of work

Research on apprenticeship, curriculum, and professional agency stays in its own lane.

This work is adjacent to the prototypes, but it has a different purpose. It asks how entry-level work, apprenticeship, judgment, and curriculum should change as AI becomes part of ordinary professional practice.

01 working paper Entry-Level Work Is Apprenticeship Infrastructure How early-career work teaches judgment, context, and professional agency. 02 working paper The Junior Analyst Problem What changes when entry-level analysis is accelerated, automated, or abstracted away. 03 working paper The Hidden Curriculum of Office Work The informal skills students learn only after entering professional environments. 04 working paper The AI Apprenticeship Audit A framework for where AI supports, weakens, or reshapes learning on the job. 05 working paper From Prompt Literacy to Professional Agency Moving beyond tool fluency toward judgment, authorship, and critical adoption.

Method

Prototype first, then decide what deserves to become a product.

  • Use research to identify where decision-making is messy, tacit, or trapped in static documents.
  • Build an interface that makes the workflow visible enough to critique.
  • Trace the data sources, assumptions, and gaps instead of hiding them behind a polished demo.
  • Let the prototype reveal what architecture, governance, and product surface the domain actually needs.
